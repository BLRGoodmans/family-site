Their Story

James MacDonald Goodman was born in 1860 in India. He and Mary Blanche Martin — born six years later in 1866 — together founded what would grow into a remarkable family spanning four generations across India and beyond.

James passed away in 1900 at just 40 years of age, leaving Mary Blanche to carry the family forward. Their son William, born in 1886, would go on to build a life on the railways of southern India and raise eight children of his own.

Mary Blanche, née Martin, lived on after James and witnessed the next generation of Goodmans grow up across the railway towns of Madras, Ranipet, Guntakal and Gadag — the India of the early twentieth century.

Though records of their lives are sparse, James and Mary Blanche are remembered as the roots from which the entire Goodman family tree grows.

"The story of the Goodmans begins here — in India, in the 1860s."

— Family record
