📸 Adding Your Own Photos

To replace the placeholders with real family photos:

  1. Add your image files to the /images/ folder in the site package
  2. In gallery.html, find the relevant gallery-item div
  3. Change data-src="" to data-src="images/your-photo.jpg"
  4. Replace the gallery-placeholder div with <img src="images/your-photo.jpg" alt="Caption">
  5. Update the data-caption text

Or use the Contribute page to let family members submit photos via email.
