🛠 Site Admin: Receiving Submissions

This form uses Netlify Forms — submissions appear automatically in your Netlify dashboard.

  1. Log in to netlify.com → open your site
  2. Click Forms in the top menu
  3. Find family-contributions to see all submissions
  4. To get email alerts: Forms → Notifications → Add email notification
  5. Download uploaded photos from each submission entry
