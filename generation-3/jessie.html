Their Story

Jessie Blanche Goodman was born on 13 June 1920 in Madras, the eldest child of William McDonald Goodman and Mary Katherine (née Eade). She grew up across the railway towns of southern India as her father's postings moved the family from Madras to Ranipet, Guntakal and Gadag.
