Their Story

William McDonald Goodman was born on 27 August 1886 in India, the son of James MacDonald Goodman and Mary Blanche (née Martin). He went on to build a distinguished career on the railways of southern India, serving as a Station Master on the Southern Railway — a role that took his young family to towns and stations across the subcontinent.

Mary Katherine Eade was born on 29 September 1898 in Bangalore. She and William were married on 15 December 1919 in Bangalore, beginning a partnership that would produce eight children born across the railway towns of Madras, Ranipet, Guntakal and Gadag over the following sixteen years.

Life as a railway family in colonial India meant frequent moves between postings. Each of their children was born in a different station town — a testament to William's career on the rails and the family's life woven into the fabric of India's great railway network.

William passed away on 9 July 1952 in Bangalore, aged 65. He is remembered as the patriarch of a large and close-knit family, and his railway career connected the family to the wider story of India in the first half of the twentieth century.

"A Station Master on the Southern Railway — William carried the family across the towns and tracks of southern India."
